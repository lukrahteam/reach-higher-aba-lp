What is ABA Therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is a science-backed approach to helping children with autism develop skills and reduce challenging behaviors. Our ABA therapy is:

  • Play-based — therapy happens through fun, natural activities
  • Data-driven — every session is tracked and adjusted based on progress
  • One-on-one — personalized to your child's unique strengths and needs
  • BCBA-designed — licensed, certified supervisors oversee everything
  • Practical — skills learned at home transfer to school, community, everywhere
  • Warm — our therapists build real relationships with your family
