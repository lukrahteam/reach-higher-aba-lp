Evidence-Based Care

What is ABA Therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis is a science-based approach focused on skills that matter in real daily life — communication, self-care, social connection, and independence. For children with autism, ABA is play-based, warm, and built entirely around your child.

  • One-on-one sessions designed and supervised by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst
  • Progress measured and tracked every week — no guesswork
  • Built around your child's natural interests, not a generic program
  • The goal is your child thriving on their own terms, not just compliance

Why In-Home

In-Home ABA & Its Benefits

In-home ABA is not the easier option. It's often the better one. Skills practiced in the real environment — your home, your routines, your family — transfer faster and last longer.

  • Skills generalize into school, community, and daily routines immediately
  • No commute, no waiting room — therapy fits your schedule and your home
  • Parents and siblings participate and learn strategies they use every day
  • Sessions adapt in real time to your child's environment and daily rhythms

A Day in the Life: What an In-Home Session Looks Like

Arrival
5 minutes

Your BCBA or RBT arrives at your home, greets your family, and settles into your child's space.

Warm-Up
10 minutes

A few minutes of play and connection to build comfort, read your child's mood, and transition into structured work.

Structured Work
60–90 minutes

BCBA-designed activities targeting your child's goals. Play-based, one-on-one, data tracked.

Natural Routine Integration
20 minutes

Therapist practices skills in real contexts — snack time, bathroom, transitions, sibling play.

Parent Coaching
15 minutes

RBT coaches you and your family on the strategies they just used. You carry progress home.

Total session time: 2 to 4 hours depending on your plan.
