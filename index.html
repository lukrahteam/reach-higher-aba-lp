What is ABA Therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is a science-based approach to understanding and improving behavior. For children with autism, ABA therapy is play-based, practical, and data-driven.

Every session is one-on-one, designed by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), and deeply human. We focus on skills that matter in real life — communication, self-care, social connection, independence. Progress is measured week by week.

ABA isn't rigid. It's warm, practical, and built around your child's natural interests and daily routines. The goal is not compliance — it's helping your child thrive on their own terms.

A Day in the Life: What an In-Home Session Looks Like

Arrival
5 minutes

Your BCBA or RBT arrives at your home, greets your family, and settles into your child's space.

Warm-Up
10 minutes

A few minutes of play and connection to build comfort, read your child's mood, and transition into structured work.

Structured Work
60–90 minutes

BCBA-designed activities targeting your child's goals. Play-based, one-on-one, data tracked.

Natural Routine Integration
20 minutes

Therapist practices skills in real contexts — snack time, bathroom, transitions, sibling play.

Parent Coaching
15 minutes

RBT coaches you and your family on the strategies they just used. You carry progress home.

Total session time: 2 to 4 hours depending on your plan.
